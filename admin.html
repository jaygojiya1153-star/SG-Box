Why it works this way

This site has no live database, so there's nothing that can charge you money and nothing that needs a server. All slot data lives in one plain file, js/slots-data.js, which your public homepage reads directly.

This tool is just a convenient form: it builds the list on this page, and when you're happy with it, it writes out the exact code for you to paste into that file on GitHub. GitHub Pages then rebuilds your site automatically, usually within a minute.

Nothing you do here saves automatically — always finish by pasting the generated code into GitHub and committing it.

Bookings & the Google Sheet

Every online booking is saved to a Google Sheet (your real, free database) via a small Google Apps Script. The Bookings tab reads straight from that sheet — same data whether you check it here or open the Sheet itself on your phone.

When a booking comes in, the slot board on the homepage does not update automatically — go to js/slots-data.js and change that slot's status to "booked" once you've confirmed it, so other visitors don't double-book it.

Full setup steps (one-time, ~10 minutes, no card needed) are in README.md.
